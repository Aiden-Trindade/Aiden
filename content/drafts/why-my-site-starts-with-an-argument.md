Most portfolios open with a job title. Mine opens with a motion: *this house believes Aiden should be your next intern.*

You can vote For. You can also try voting Against, but the button runs away from you, and if you do catch it you get overruled.

## Why a debate

I did competitive debate in school and won at state level. It taught me to explain an idea under pressure, and that's most of what a portfolio has to do. You get a few seconds with someone, and you have to make your case.

So I made the case literally.
